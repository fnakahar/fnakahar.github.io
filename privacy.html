Privacy policy for ui-daily

Last updated: 29 October 2026

ui-daily is used only by its developer. It accesses the developer's own Google Drive to save files it creates, and the developer's own Gmail account to send messages to the developer.

  • It does not access data of any other person.
  • It does not share, sell or transfer any data to third parties.
  • The access credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository owned by the developer.
  • The developer can revoke access at any time in the Google Account settings.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
